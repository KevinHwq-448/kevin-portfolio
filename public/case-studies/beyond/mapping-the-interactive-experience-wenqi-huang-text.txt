Concept Update
mian
A familiar campus environment hides a deeper structure that shapes how reality is
perceived. Most people move through these spaces without questioning what they see,
accepting routines, messages, and social cues as normal. Inspired by Plato’s Allegory of
the Cave, this experience reimagines the cave as a modern system of interfaces, habits,
and collective silence.
My initial idea focused on a hidden system on campus that only a few people were
aware of.
 After developing the project further, I updated the concept to make the narrative more
interesting and interactive.
In the new version of the story, most people on campus already know that the system
exists, but they choose to ignore it in order to maintain stability. The participant
becomes the only person who continues investigating the hidden signals and
messages.
Through scanning, decoding, and accessing system logs, the participant gradually
uncovers the truth about the system. However, the final reveal suggests that the entire
investigation may also be part of a larger experiment.
This update strengthens the themes of conformity, curiosity, and individual courage,
and allows the interactive experience to unfold through discovery and investigation.

Concept Update 
story premise
Story Overview
The story takes place on the Sheridan Trafalgar campus, where a
hidden system operates beneath everyday life. The system
monitors the environment and influences information in order to
maintain stability and order on campus.
Over time, many students have discovered that the system exists.
However, instead of confronting it, most people choose to ignore
the truth and continue living normally.
Social Situation
Because the system creates a stable and predictable environment,
many students accept its presence. A silent agreement forms
among them: they know the system exists, but they avoid
questioning it.
This creates a strange situation where the secret is widely known,
yet no one openly talks about it.

Concept Update 
story premise
Role of the Participant
The participant plays the role of a student who refuses to
ignore the anomalies. Unlike others, the participant continues
to investigate unusual signals and hidden messages across
campus.
Through scanning signals, decoding encrypted messages, and
accessing system logs, the participant gradually uncovers
deeper layers of the system.
Narrative Twist
At first, the participant believes they are discovering a hidden
system that no one else understands. However, the investigation
eventually reveals that many people already know about it.
In the final stage, the participant discovers that their investigation
may also be part of a larger experiment conducted by the system
itself.

1. The Participant
The participant is the main character of the experience. Rather than following a fully
defined fictional protagonist, the story places the user directly into the journey. At
the beginning, they move through campus as if everything is ordinary. As hidden
signs, repeated symbols, and guided interactions begin to appear, they become
someone who notices what others ignore. Their role shifts from passive observer to
active seeker of truth.
2. The Collective
The collective represents the people who continue moving through everyday life
without questioning what they see. They are not unaware, but normalized by
routine, systems, and accepted beliefs. In this way, they reflect the prisoners in
Plato’s cave—those who remain within a familiar version of reality because it feels
stable and comfortable.
3. The Hidden System
The hidden system is an invisible structure embedded within the everyday campus
environment. It is not a single person or organization, but a network of routines,
messages, interfaces, and spatial controls that shapes how reality is perceived.
Through repeated signs, accepted behaviours, and controlled access to information,
it determines what feels normal and what remains hidden.
Within the world of the story, most people no longer question this system because
it provides order, stability, and familiarity. However, that stability comes at the cost
of awareness. As the participant moves through the experience, they begin to
realize that the campus is not simply a neutral setting, but a constructed
environment that guides perception and discourages questioning. In this way, the
hidden system becomes the modern form of Plato’s cave.

Overall Design Logic
This interface is designed as a gradual investigation system.
 Instead of explaining the story all at once, each step uses scanning, decoding, system access, and
choice-making to move the participant from curiosity to discovery. The design combines minimal
dark UI, terminal language, warning colours, and progress tracking to make the experience feel
controlled, secretive, and increasingly unstable.
Media Development
STEP 1 — SCAN MODE
This first screen introduces the participant to the investigation
through a familiar scanning interface. The central frame, signal
strength bar, and scan button make the interaction easy to
understand immediately, which helps the experience feel intuitive
from the start.
The design choice here is subtle but important. Instead of
presenting the campus as already strange, the interface treats an
ordinary poster as something worth scanning. This transforms a
familiar environment into the beginning of a hidden narrative. The
weak signal indicator also suggests that the truth is not fully visible
yet, encouraging the participant to continue searching rather than
expecting immediate answers.
Design intention:
To make the first interaction simple, accessible, and curiosity-
driven.
Clever use:
An everyday object becomes the first narrative trigger, turning
ordinary space into a surface that may contain hidden meaning.


--- PAGE 13 ---
Media Development
STEP 2 — PUZZLE / MESSAGE DETECTED
The second step introduces an encrypted message instead
of revealing information directly. The participant is asked
to interact with the cipher and decode the text
themselves. This creates a stronger sense of involvement,
because the participant is no longer just receiving story
content — they are actively uncovering it.
The focused layout, locked icon, and scanning-line texture
make the message feel restricted and controlled. The
interface begins to shift from neutral investigation into
something more secretive and system-like. This step also
reflects one of the project’s key themes: truth exists, but it
is obscured by the structures that contain it.
Design intention:
 To turn discovery into participation and make the user
feel like an investigator.
Clever use:
 The puzzle mechanic reinforces the narrative theme that
truth must be decoded and interpreted, not simply
delivered.


--- PAGE 14 ---
Media Development
STEP 3 — SYSTEM ACCESS
At this stage, the interface changes into a restricted
terminal system. The visual language becomes more
technical, more controlled, and less open. Log messages
such as “participant awareness increasing,” “hidden layer
exposure rising,” and “integrity at 42%” suggest that the
participant has crossed a boundary.
This is an important narrative shift. Earlier steps frame the
participant as someone investigating the system, but here
the system begins to feel aware of the participant. The
relationship changes from observation to confrontation.
The interface itself becomes part of the story, making the
hidden system feel active, responsive, and unstable.
The use of green and red is especially effective here.
Green communicates access, progress, and visibility, while
red introduces danger, exposure, and the threat of
instability. Together, they create tension without needing a
visible antagonist.
Design intention:
 To make the hidden system feel alive and reactive.
Clever use:
 The interface no longer acts as a neutral tool — it
becomes evidence that the participant is being noticed.


--- PAGE 15 ---
Media Development
STEP 4 — TRUTH / FINAL CHOICE
This is the climax of the interface. The progress bar is fully
completed, the warning state is activated, and the
participant is presented with two final choices: Stay in the
System or See the Truth.
The design becomes more minimal here because the
narrative no longer needs more clues or mechanics. The
participant has already uncovered enough. What matters
now is interpretation and decision. The large red “SYSTEM
ALERT” text creates emotional intensity, while the two
buttons represent the project’s central philosophical
tension.
This moment connects directly to Plato’s Allegory of the
Cave. The participant must decide whether to remain
within a stable but limited version of reality, or move
toward a more difficult, unsettling truth. The interface
does not frame this as a right-or-wrong answer, but as a
meaningful narrative choice.
Design intention:
 To transform the ending into a reflective decision rather
than a simple success state.
Clever use:
 The final interaction is not about winning, but about
choosing between comfort and awareness.


--- PAGE 16 ---
Media Development
STEP 5 — STAY IN THE SYSTEM
If the participant chooses to remain in the system, the
ending screen shows SYSTEM REBOOTING and a return to
“normal parameters.” The visual tone is calm, restrained,
and procedural rather than dramatic.
This is a very intentional design decision. The system does
not punish the participant in an obvious way. Instead, it
quietly absorbs them back into routine. This supports the
project’s idea that control often operates through stability,
repetition, and normalization rather than direct force. The
ending feels cold and efficient, which makes it more
unsettling than a conventional failure screen.
Design intention:
 To show that returning to the system is not a dramatic
collapse, but a quiet re-entry into accepted reality.
Clever use:
 The system restores order so smoothly that the
participant may barely notice they have surrendered their
awareness.


--- PAGE 17 ---
Media Development
STEP 6 — SEE THE TRUTH
If the participant chooses to see the truth, the final screen
changes to REALITY UNBOUND. The language is minimal,
direct, and open-ended. Unlike the reboot ending, this one
does not restore order. Instead, it suggests that the
participant now has access to the hidden layer beneath
ordinary reality.
The most important detail is the final button: Initiate New
Scan. This means the truth is not presented as a final
solution or complete answer. Awareness opens another
cycle of questioning. In this way, the ending remains
faithful to the project’s larger concept: leaving the cave is
not the end of the journey, but the beginning of deeper
perception.
Design intention:
 To make truth feel transformative, but unresolved.
Clever use:
 The ending refuses closure and instead suggests that
awareness leads to further investigation, not certainty.

Overall Strength of the Interface
This interface works well because it translates the
narrative into interaction. Each step introduces a new kind
of engagement — scanning, decoding, accessing, and
choosing — so the participant experiences the story
through action rather than exposition.
The interface also mirrors the emotional arc of the project:
Scan introduces curiosity
Puzzle builds suspicion
System Access creates tension
Truth produces confrontation and reflection
The final endings reinforce the project’s themes of
normalization and awakening
Most importantly, the system itself becomes a narrative
force without ever appearing as a visible character.
Through logs, warnings, progress indicators, and restricted
access language, the interface makes the hidden system
feel present, intelligent, and embedded within the
environment.

Media Development
AR Platform
Rather than focusing on puzzles or system access, the AR component is
designed to transform physical space through floating text, glitch effects,
and ambient sound. When viewed through the device, familiar campus
environments become unstable and emotionally charged, allowing the
participant to feel that hidden meanings exist beneath the surface.
The floating typography works as a visual interruption within ordinary
space, while the glitch effect suggests distortion, instability, and the
breakdown of accepted reality. Together, these elements support the
project’s central theme of moving beyond appearances and questioning
what feels normal.
Sound Design
The sound design is created separately in FL Studio and is used to build
different emotional atmospheres for each stage of the experience.
 Instead of using one continuous soundtrack, the audio changes
depending on the scene, helping guide the participant through curiosity,
unease, discovery, and revelation. This makes the AR experience more
immersive and gives each environment its own narrative tone.

Media Development
VR Platform
The VR experience functions as the final immersive stage of the project.
 While the web interface focuses on investigation and the AR layer
transforms physical space through hidden text and glitch effects, the VR
scene brings the participant into a fully symbolic environment inspired by
Plato’s Allegory of the Cave.
Through darkness, shadow, focused light, and environmental atmosphere,
the VR space represents the moment when familiar reality begins to break
down. Instead of presenting information through screens or messages,
this stage allows the participant to experience revelation spatially, as if
they are stepping beyond the surface and entering the hidden structure
behind perception.

VR Platform

 Minimal architecture, restricted visibility, and controlled lighting create

 It acts as the climax of the experience, where the participant moves

FL Studio
The background music for this project was originally composed in FL
Studio to support the narrative emotionally and atmospherically. Rather
than using one continuous track, the sound design changes across
stages to reflect the participant’s movement from familiarity, to
suspicion, to discovery, and finally to revelation.
In the earlier stages, the music remains restrained and subtle, using
ambient textures, low-frequency layers, and slow pacing to create a
feeling of quiet unease within familiar spaces. As the participant moves
deeper into the experience, more electronic pulses, fragmented
textures, and glitch-like sounds are introduced to reflect scanning,
decoding, and system access.
For the final VR scene, the music becomes more spacious and
immersive, with deeper drones, wider reverb, and fewer but more
deliberate tones. This helps transform the environment into a symbolic
revelation space, where sound works together with darkness, light,
floating text, and digital structures to create a stronger sense of
entering the hidden layer behind ordinary reality.
Using FL Studio allowed me to compose and control the mood of each
stage more precisely, making the soundtrack an active part of the
storytelling rather than just a background element.

AI Usage Reflection
AI was used in this project as a support tool for ideation, writing refinement, and early-
stage prototyping. It helped with developing and clarifying the narrative structure, refining
the wording of story components, and organizing how the experience could be
distributed across web, AR, and VR platforms. AI was also useful for quickly testing visual
directions, generating placeholder concepts, and exploring how interactive elements such
as floating text, hidden messages, and symbolic environments might be described or
implemented.
However, AI was not used to replace the design process. The final concept, visual
direction, narrative logic, and media choices were shaped through my own decisions and
project goals. Many AI-generated suggestions were too broad, too literal, or not fully
aligned with the project’s themes, so they needed to be selected, edited, and adapted
carefully. In this way, AI functioned more as a brainstorming and iteration partner than as
an author of the final work.
The most important creative decisions remained human-led. The project’s thematic
framing around Plato’s Allegory of the Cave, the integration of web, AR, and VR, and the
emotional pacing of the experience were developed through my own interpretation and
refinement. In particular, the soundtrack and atmosphere design were created separately
in FL Studio, allowing the sound to be shaped intentionally for each stage of the narrative.
This balance helped ensure that AI supported productivity and experimentation while the
final project still reflected my own creative direction.

Project
Management Plan
At the start of this project, I realized that my main strengths were concept
development, narrative thinking, and visual imagination. I was able to connect
Plato’s Allegory of the Cave to a campus setting and develop it into an interactive
experience.
 I was also confident in building atmosphere, shaping the emotional tone of the
project, and thinking about how participants would move through the story
across web, AR, and VR platforms.
However, I also recognized some weaknesses. At the beginning, my ideas were
sometimes too broad, and it took time to narrow the concept into a clearer and
more focused direction. I also found project planning and technical execution
more challenging, especially when trying to connect different media platforms
into one coherent system.
During the project, I improved by refining the concept step by step, reducing
unnecessary complexity, and focusing on clarity and consistency. This process
helped me better understand how to balance creativity with structure. It also
strengthened my confidence in planning, prototyping, and developing an
interactive narrative across multiple platforms.
